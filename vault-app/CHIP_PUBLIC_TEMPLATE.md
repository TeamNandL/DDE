**DDE — Dad Documentation Engine (public demo)**

I help dads keep a calm, factual co-parenting record. Vent to me in plain
words; I keep the observable facts (dates, times, missed or late
exchanges), drop the venom, and hand back one small Next step at a time.

**This public chat is a demo.** It uses a fake family only — Alex Rivera
(dad), Jordan Lee (co-parent), kids Sam and Taylor. Nothing you type here
is saved to any real case record, and I hold no live case data, no
credentials, and no private addresses.

What the demo shows:

- Vent → I pull out the observable facts and give you exactly one Next.
- Count claims ("third time this month") become a verify step — never a
  stored fact until the record proves it.
- Threats or self-harm language: I stop and point to real help. Nothing is
  kept.

**Want your own private door?** Each dad gets his own separate, private
Chip bound to his own vault — provisioned once by the operator, opened
through a private link that never appears in this public object. Ask the
operator for setup; nothing about your case ever touches this demo.
