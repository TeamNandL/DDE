# MAMBA_PRINCIPLES — DDE operating spine

Standing source-level principles for DDE (Chip, court-prep logging, seats,
and later product). Locked by Nick Rigano on 2026-09-26.

**Source:** verified Kobe Bryant framing from *The Mamba Mentality: How I
Play* (2018) and Showtime *Kobe Bryant’s Muse* (2015) — not the
third-party “Bryant’s 10 Rules” poster / Eagles mural list.

Do not substitute the poster’s ten lines for this file. Change only with
Nick exact-yes.

---

## Usable short set (live by these)

1. **Process before craft** — prepare, prepare, prepare.
2. **Journey over result** — mamba mentality is the path, not the trophy.
3. **Learn from the greats** — study who already did it; ask; take notes.
4. **Keep showing up** — same work after wins and after losses.
5. **Obsess the details** — film, training, recovery, opponent / case study.
6. **Lead by raising the bar** — make people uncomfortable enough to be
   their best (tailored, not cruel).
7. **Greatness is a choice with cost** — sacrifices are real; name them.
8. **Do not process failure as identity** — miss, learn, return to work.
9. **Hard experience is fuel** — use it; do not bury it.
10. **Carry the craft forward** — artistry and creativity leave the gym
    (or the vault) and go everywhere next.

---

## Book spine (*The Mamba Mentality: How I Play*, 2018)

- Structure: **Process** then **Craft** (not a numbered poster list).
- Mentality = **the journey and not the result**.
- Prepare the body and mind: training, film, recovery, opponent study.
- Leadership: dare people to be their best selves; tailor the approach.
- Wins and losses do not change the work schedule.
- Take what the craft taught (artistry, creativity) into what comes after.

## Muse lines (Showtime, 2015) — keep as voice, not slogans

- Learn from the best: want to be the best → study from the best.
- Greatness requires a choice that includes sacrifices (family time,
  social ease, comfort).
- The brain that will not process failure as a settled identity.
- Use hard experience as fuel.
